Who we are

Cowoka is a mobile app operated by the Cowoka project. Product experience lives in the app (tabs: Home, Explore, Pulse, Account). This marketing site is getcowoka.com.

Privacy questions: privacy@getcowoka.com (placeholder until a monitored inbox is ready).

Data we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Account data — email, display name / nickname, and related authentication identifiers when you sign up or sign in (including Google Sign-In where offered).
  • Profile content — bio, vibe status, and other fields you choose to save on your profile.
  • App activity — venues you add or favorite, reviews, co-work sessions you create or join, and similar community content.
  • Device / location — approximate or precise location when you grant permission (for map discovery on Explore / nearby venues); otherwise optional city selection stored locally on device.
  • Diagnostics — crash and error reports on non-development builds when Crashlytics (or similar) is enabled for that flavor.

How we use data

We use this information to operate Cowoka: authentication, showing venues and sessions near you, community features (including Pulse and Home activity), and improving reliability. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

We rely on infrastructure providers (for example Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Crashlytics) to run the app. Content you publish in the community (venues, reviews, public sessions) may be visible to other users.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the Account tab in the app (when that feature is available in your build). Deletion removes your auth user and profile document; related cleanup (for example sessions you organized) is handled by backend jobs. Community reviews or venues you contributed may remain as part of shared product content.

Children

Cowoka is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).
